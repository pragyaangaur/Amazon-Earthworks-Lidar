# Review of the Open Math OS swarm run (13 to 17 November 2026)

This review was written on 19 November 2026 after reading the returned archive, the validation files and the chat log, and after looking at every candidate image pack and every under-forest control. It holds no coordinates.

## What the swarm did

The swarm ran for about four and a half days on Open Math OS. It built 3 m ground models for 899 of the 901 EBA transects in nine states. Two transects (0082 and 0865) have no file in the Zenodo archive. It screened every transect for ring ditches, mound clusters and raised lines, and it examined 3,537 features. It rejected 3,494 of them with written reasons, kept 42 as Possible and confirmed the 0414 control as Known. It made no Strong candidate and no claim of a new site.

The swarm also built a test bench for long sunken roads with synthetic ditches, held-out cases and natural confounders. The detector failed that bench, so the long-road screen was never switched on for real data, and the swarm marked no transect as fully done for that reason. Members reported their own errors several times, and the coordinator rejected inflated recall figures and fixed them before packaging. The archive passed CRC, content and checklist audits.

## The positive control

The 0414 geoglyph is a closed circular ditch about 98 m across with about 1.3 m of relief. It matches the register entry "Acre Circle 90m" within a few metres. My earlier estimate of 40 m was wrong.

## The under-forest problem

Five transects contain TREES/INPE points from Peripato et al. 2023, which are earthworks that study found under forest canopy. At all five, the swarm's detector could read almost none of the ground around the points (0 to 0.2 percent of a 100 m window), and more than half of the ground cells there were reconstructed rather than observed. I looked at the hillshade, relief and canopy images for all five, and no ditch or ring is visible at any of them. The relief under tall forest is speckle at this point density.

This means the method has never been shown to work under forest. Either the EBA point density and this ground filter cannot show these earthworks, or the published TREES/INPE positions are too far displaced to test. The images cannot tell these apart. A result of "nothing found" under forest therefore says nothing about whether earthworks are there.

## My view of the 42 Possible candidates

I looked at the hillshade and relief image of every candidate. Most have a plain explanation. The mound clusters are mostly gully heads, hummocky ground or stream junctions. Several raised lines are plough lines, field plots, modern roads or processing stripes, and one ring sits on a block-shaped processing artefact. The swarm's own duplicate audit also found that four Possible rows are twins of rejected rows.

A small group is not explained by the images alone. These are short (about 100 m) straight raised lines under 10 to 23 m of forest in Amazonas and Mato Grosso (0825-CW001, 0229-CW007, 0228-CW004, 0202-CW002, 1012-CW005), and a flat surface in Pará dotted with small regular bumps (M0657-006). Natural levees, old forest tracks and natural earth mounds can all produce these shapes, and none of them looks like a geoglyph. I rate all of them as Possible with low weight, which agrees with the swarm.

## Is any of it useful

The archive is useful as infrastructure and as a negative result. It contains:

- 3 m ground models for 899 transects, which will save about four days of downloads in any later attempt;
- a documented record check against Peripato, the Jacobs KML and the 2026 Pärssinen survey;
- a measured statement of what this method cannot see.

It does not contain a discovery. There is no candidate strong enough to send to IPHAN or to an archaeologist as a likely new site, and the draft letter in the archive should not be sent in its current form.

## What would change the result

1. Get an under-forest control that works. The best route is to ask the Peripato authors for the true positions or images of two or three TREES/INPE sites, or to find a published geoglyph under forest inside a transect, and then test whether any ground filter can show it in the EBA points.
2. If no filter can show it, stop searching under forest with this data and say so. The open-ground parts of the transects were searched and only the known site was found.
3. If a filter can show it, rerun only the ring screen on the forested parts of the Acre, Rondônia and southern Amazonas transects with that filter.
